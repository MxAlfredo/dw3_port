stage-tiles
===========

What it changes
  The map tiles of Central Park (stage 330) turn purple (palette only).

Where to see it
  Walk in Central Park: the ground and buildings.

How it is made
  Stage tile mod: z_stage/s330pack/, made at install time from your exported stage (settings.ini [data] export_stages = S330).

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
