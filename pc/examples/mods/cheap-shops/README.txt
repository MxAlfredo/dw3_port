cheap-shops
===========

What it changes
  Every item costs half as much in the item shops (buy price; selling price unchanged).

Where to see it
  Any item shop, e.g. the first item shop (shop 0, in Asuka): open BUY and compare the prices.

How it is made
  Table patch: tables/item_prices.json

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
