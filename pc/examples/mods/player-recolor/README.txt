player-recolor
==============

What it changes
  The player character's blue clothes turn red (palette only).

Where to see it
  Walk around in Central Park or any field stage: the protagonist's cap and clothes.

How it is made
  Image mod (field sprites p001play, p002play and its pose files p002plyd, p002plyf, p002plyk, p002plyl: falling, minigame, searching, climbing), made at install time from your exported sprites.

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
