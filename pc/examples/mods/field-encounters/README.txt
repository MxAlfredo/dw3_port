field-encounters
================

What it changes
  The two first Central Park enemies swap places (encounters 34 and 35) and random battles come twice as often.

Where to see it
  Walk in Central Park (stage 330): battles come after half the steps, and the two first enemies swap places (the same two enemy rows, in the other order).

How it is made
  Table patches: tables/field_enemies.json, tables/battle_rates.json. Do not enable together with sample-enemy.

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
