sample-card
===========

What it changes
  Adds a new Digimon card with its own art, sold in card shop 49, in booster 91 and played by one rival.

Where to see it
  Card shop 49 (the first card shop): Buy One Card, sixth card of the row; the card album; a duel against the rival on table row 2 (Asuka City opponent 3).

How it is made
  Content mod: content/cards.json, card/test-card.png and card tables

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
