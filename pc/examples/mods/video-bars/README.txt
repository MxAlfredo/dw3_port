video-bars
==========

What it changes
  Replaces the opening movie with a 3 second colour-bars clip with a rising tone.

Where to see it
  The opening movie that plays when the game starts.

How it is made
  Video mod: video/MOVIEOPN.avi and MOVIEOP2.avi, a synthetic MJPEG AVI generated at install time (needs Pillow).

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
