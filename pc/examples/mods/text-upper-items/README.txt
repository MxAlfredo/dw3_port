text-upper-items
================

What it changes
  The item names are written in capital letters.

Where to see it
  The item shop lists, the item bag and the battle item menu.

How it is made
  Text mod. The files are made at install time from your exported text/<language>/ssitmnam.json.

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
