sample-enemy
============

What it changes
  Adds a new enemy, Sproutmon, and places it in two Central Park random battles.

Where to see it
  Random battles in Central Park (stage 330): the second enemy slot of encounters 34 and 35.

How it is made
  Content mod: content/digimon.json. Do not enable together with sample-model or field-encounters (they use the same encounters).

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
