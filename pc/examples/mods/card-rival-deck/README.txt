card-rival-deck
===============

What it changes
  Every card rival gets ten copies of one card (card 120) in the first 10 of its 40 deck places, and shows level 99.
  The game shuffles only the player's deck; a rival draws its deck in table order, so it always opens with those
  ten copies and then draws its original cards (places 11-40). The 9-copy limit is for the player's collection,
  not for rival decks.

Where to see it
  Any card duel: the opponent's first draws are all the same card; later draws are its usual cards.

How it is made
  Table patch: tables/card_opponents.json (card_opponents).

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
