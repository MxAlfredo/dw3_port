sample-digimon
==============

What it changes
  Makes partner form 383 (the starting Kotemon) stronger with a new technique, makes enemy 4 tougher and adds a new partner form, Brasamon.

Where to see it
  Status screen of the starting partner (stats), the Tech menu in battle, and the Digimon lab.

How it is made
  Content mod: content/digimon.json, content/techs.json

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
