gym-inn-prices
==============

What it changes
  Every inn night costs 1 BIT per partner, and the gym's two higher intensities cost about half the training points (5 -> 3 and 10 -> 5; the lowest stays 1).

Where to see it
  An inn (talk to the innkeeper and pay the night), and the gym's training screen (intensity cost).

How it is made
  Table patches: tables/inns.json, tables/gym_intensity_costs.json

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
