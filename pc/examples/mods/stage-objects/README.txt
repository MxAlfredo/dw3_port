stage-objects
=============

What it changes
  The map-object art of stage 233 (a blue circuit-floor stage) turns purple (palette only): the big screen above the platform and other small objects.

Where to see it
  Go to stage 233 (warp token warp:233) and look at the big screen over the central platform. Central Park has no object art worth showing, so this one uses another stage.

How it is made
  Stage object mod: images/field/stage/s233mask/, made at install time from your exported images.

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
