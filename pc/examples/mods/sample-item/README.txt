sample-item
===========

What it changes
  Adds a new consumable, Test Gadget (heals 300 HP), sold in shop 0 and dropped by every enemy.

Where to see it
  the first item shop (shop 0, in Asuka), the end of the BUY list; also after any battle, and in the item bag.

How it is made
  Content mod: content/items.json

Install: pc/examples/install_examples.sh copies it into your mods/ folder (switched off), then
enable it in Options > Mods and restart. Remove it by deleting its folder.
Nothing here comes from the game: files that need game data are generated on your machine.
